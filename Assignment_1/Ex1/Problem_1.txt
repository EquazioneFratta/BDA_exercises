[ ANALYSIS ]
------------------------------------------------------------
Execution time scales linearly with input size.
Each additional subdirectory adds approximately ~27 seconds.

This is consistent with a MapReduce pipeline where work grows 
proportionally to input, with no significant overhead 
introduced by larger datasets.

Also, 1.b.1 and 1.d.2 had no results if runned only on the
first folder.


The words which have frequency of exactly 1000 considering
all files are:
- ordering
- capturing
- carthage

There are no pairs of words which have exactly 1000 entries.
2010-census is the number-word most frequent pair
(1381 occurrences).
"during" is the most frequent word, with 107826 occurrences
